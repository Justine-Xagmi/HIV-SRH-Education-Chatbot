# R05.4 — Topic Prioritization and Document Inventory

**Project:** HIV/SRH Education Chatbot  
**Milestone:** R05 — Knowledge Content Preparation and Source Verification  
**Checkpoint:** R05.4  
**Status:** Approved scope; inventory ready for documentation

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes the priority order of the chatbot's initial educational knowledge areas and maps candidate publications to the planned knowledge documents.

It also defines the initial English and Kiswahili preparation requirements.

This inventory does not approve source permissions, clinical content, translations, or documents for chatbot retrieval.

## 5. Minimum Preparation Requirements

Every planned knowledge document must:

1. Have a clearly defined educational purpose and intended audience.
2. Identify its supporting publication or publications.
3. Include traceable source references and relevant publication details.
4. Use clear, accessible language suitable for a general audience.
5. Avoid unsupported claims, invented statistics and fabricated citations.
6. Avoid diagnosing users or providing individualized prescriptions.
7. Include appropriate educational safety notices and referral guidance where supported by verified sources.
8. Be checked for consistency with its supporting sources.
9. Undergo qualified clinical review before approval.
10. Have its English or Kiswahili version reviewed independently for meaning and clarity.

## 6. English and Kiswahili Preparation Strategy

English and Kiswahili documents will be maintained as separate knowledge records.

The Kiswahili versions must preserve the intended meaning of the reviewed English content or be independently developed from the same authoritative source material.

Clinical terminology must be checked for accuracy and understandable local usage.

Machine translation alone will not constitute approval.

## 7. Review and Approval Statuses

Each knowledge document will use a controlled preparation status:

- Not prepared
- Draft prepared
- Source verification pending
- Language review pending
- Clinical review pending
- Approved
- Rejected
- Retired

Only documents with the required source, permission, language and clinical approvals may proceed to the approved knowledge base.

## 8. Outstanding Decisions and Dependencies

Before preparation and ingestion:

- Confirm document-level reuse and translation permissions.
- Obtain and inspect the full source publications.
- Confirm the applicable currency of clinical recommendations.
- Identify qualified clinical reviewers.
- Establish the review process for English and Kiswahili documents.
- Confirm whether additional publications are required to adequately support the seven initial knowledge areas.

## 9. Future Expansion

Broader sexual health education beyond the seven initial knowledge areas will be considered in a separate expansion milestone.

That expansion will be planned after the initial core content has been prepared and reviewed. It will require its own topic assessment, source mapping, language preparation and clinical review.

It is not part of the current 14-document inventory.
