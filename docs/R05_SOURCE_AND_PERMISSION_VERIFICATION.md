## 5\. Reuse and Permission Assessment

### 5.1 WHO publications

The official WHO publication records for DOC-002, DOC-003, DOC-005, DOC-007, DOC-008, DOC-010 and DOC-011 identify the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0 IGO licence.

The licence generally permits copying, redistribution, translation and adaptation for non-commercial purposes, subject to its conditions.

Requirements include:

* Appropriate attribution.
* Compliance with share-alike terms for adaptations.
* No implication of WHO endorsement.
* No unauthorized use of the WHO logo.
* Separate assessment of third-party material.
* Permission from WHO for commercial use.

The licence must be checked against the exact PDF and any material reproduced from it.

### 5.2 Tanzanian national publications

DOC-001 and DOC-012 have official document locations.

However, an official download location does not automatically grant permission to reproduce, translate, adapt or redistribute the full document.

Their applicable reuse terms must be established before ingestion.

### 5.3 UNFPA Tanzania publication

DOC-009 is available through the official UNFPA Tanzania publication page and downloadable PDF.

The applicable reuse and adaptation terms still require verification.

## 6\. Language Availability

The official records reviewed establish the existence of the identified publications but do not yet provide sufficient evidence to confirm that all 12 documents are available in both English and Kiswahili.

For every selected document, the project must:

1. Inspect the official download page.
2. Check available language editions.
3. Record the exact edition and language.
4. Determine whether translation is permitted.
5. Obtain appropriate human and clinical review for translated content.

Machine translation alone does not establish clinical approval.

## 7\. Source Currency and Local Applicability

The following considerations must be applied:

* Tanzania national guidance should be assessed before adopting international recommendations as local operational guidance.
* WHO publications from 2024–2026 should be considered alongside applicable Tanzanian national guidance.
* The 2021 WHO consolidated HIV guideline must be checked against subsequent updates.
* The Tanzania HIV Impact Survey is population-level evidence, not an individualized clinical reference.
* WHO maternal and contraceptive recommendations require qualified review and appropriate local adaptation.
