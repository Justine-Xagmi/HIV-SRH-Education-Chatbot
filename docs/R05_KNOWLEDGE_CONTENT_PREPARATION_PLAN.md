# R05.1 — Knowledge Content Preparation Plan

**Project:** HIV/SRH Education Chatbot  
**Milestone:** R05 — Knowledge Content Preparation and Source Verification  
**Checkpoint:** R05.1  
**Status:** Proposed plan for project implementation  
**Languages:** English and Kiswahili

## 1\. Purpose

This document establishes the plan for preparing reliable, understandable, bilingual educational content for the HIV/SRH Education Chatbot.

The chatbot must provide educational information based on verified, permitted, and appropriately reviewed knowledge documents.

The purpose of R05 is to establish a trustworthy knowledge base rather than rely on unsupported model-generated medical information.

## 2\. Knowledge Content Scope

The initial knowledge base will cover seven educational areas.

|ID|Knowledge area|Intended coverage|
|-|-|-|
|K01|HIV prevention|Prevention methods, risk reduction and relevant educational information|
|K02|HIV testing|Testing options, testing procedures, window periods and understanding results|
|K03|HIV treatment|Treatment education, ART awareness, adherence and general treatment information|
|K04|HIV stigma and discrimination|Stigma awareness, discrimination, rights and supportive communication|
|K05|Sexual health|Sexual wellbeing, safer sex and relevant health education|
|K06|Contraception and family planning|Contraceptive methods, reproductive choices and family-planning education|
|K07|Pregnancy and other STIs|Pregnancy-related reproductive education and sexually transmitted infections other than HIV|

The seven areas form the initial content scope. Additional topics must be considered through a separately documented scope decision.

## 3\. Target Audience

The initial content is intended for general audiences seeking accessible HIV and sexual and reproductive health education.

Content should:

* Use understandable language.
* Avoid unnecessary medical terminology.
* Explain medical terms when they are necessary.
* Respect different backgrounds and circumstances.
* Avoid stigmatizing or discriminatory language.
* Encourage appropriate professional healthcare support when necessary.

## 4\. Source Selection Criteria

Every proposed knowledge document must be assessed against the following criteria:

1. **Authority:** The source must be an identifiable and credible health organization or qualified publisher.
2. **Relevance:** The material must directly address an approved chatbot topic.
3. **Currency:** Publication date, update date and clinical relevance must be assessed.
4. **Traceability:** The original document and its source URL must be identifiable.
5. **Permission:** Reuse, storage, adaptation and translation permissions must be established.
6. **Language:** The document must be available in English or Kiswahili, or have a documented translation plan.
7. **Clinical suitability:** The content must be suitable for educational use and undergo qualified clinical review.

A document that does not satisfy mandatory requirements must not be approved for chatbot retrieval.

## 5\. Candidate Source Organizations

The following organizations will be considered during source identification:

* Tanzania Ministry of Health and relevant national HIV programme structures.
* National AIDS Control Programme and other relevant national health authorities.
* World Health Organization (WHO).
* United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA).
* Other recognized public-health organizations where appropriate.

These are candidate source organizations, not automatically approved sources. Each individual document requires verification.

## 6\. Document Preparation Requirements

Every candidate knowledge document should have:

* A unique document identifier.
* A clear title.
* A primary topic and relevant additional topics.
* A language designation.
* The original source organization.
* A source URL or traceable reference.
* Publication date, where available.
* Last-updated date, where available.
* Retrieval or verification date.
* Permission evidence.
* A document version or edition.
* A content fingerprint or equivalent integrity reference.
* A clinical review status.
* A documented approval decision where applicable.

The established R04 knowledge document standard remains the structural reference.

## 7\. English and Kiswahili Content Strategy

The knowledge base will support both English and Kiswahili.

English documents may serve as original source material where appropriate. Kiswahili documents should use accurate, understandable terminology suitable for the intended Tanzanian audience.

Translation must preserve:

* Clinical meaning.
* Medical terminology.
* Safety instructions.
* Important limitations.
* Source references.
* Relevant context.

Machine translation alone must not be treated as clinical approval. Translated material must undergo appropriate human and clinical review.

## 8\. Clinical Review and Approval

The content preparation workflow will follow this sequence:

1. Identify a candidate source document.
2. Verify the source and publication information.
3. Establish permission evidence.
4. Prepare the candidate knowledge document.
5. Validate metadata, language and topics.
6. Submit the document for qualified clinical review.
7. Record the review decision and supporting rationale.
8. Approve or reject the document.
9. Make approved content eligible for knowledge-base ingestion and retrieval.

Only documents that satisfy the established approval requirements may become available to normal chatbot retrieval.

The ingestion process must never be treated as clinical approval.

## 9\. Content Safety Requirements

The knowledge base must not be used to:

* Diagnose an individual.
* Prescribe individualized medication or treatment.
* Replace professional medical assessment.
* Provide unsupported clinical claims.
* Invent citations, medical contacts or referral availability.
* Present outdated guidance as current.
* Promote stigma or discrimination.

Content must explain when users should seek appropriate professional healthcare assistance.

Urgent health concerns must be handled according to an appropriately reviewed safety and escalation policy.

## 10\. Quality Assurance

Each prepared document must undergo checks for:

* Source authenticity.
* Permission evidence.
* Topic classification.
* Language accuracy.
* Completeness of metadata.
* Duplicate content.
* Document integrity.
* Clinical review status.
* Consistency between English and Kiswahili versions.

Testing must distinguish between technically valid documents and clinically approved documents.

## 11\. R05 Implementation Sequence

|Checkpoint|Description|
|-|-|
|R05.1|Knowledge content preparation plan|
|R05.2|Identify authoritative educational documents|
|R05.3|Verify source details and reuse permissions|

|R05.6|Prepare Kiswahili knowledge documents|
|R05.7|Complete qualified clinical review and approval|
|R05.8|Ingest approved documents into the knowledge base|
|R05.9|Evaluate retrieval quality and answer accuracy|

Each checkpoint requires verification and explicit project-owner approval before proceeding.

## 12\. Completion Criteria

R05.1 will be considered complete when:

* The initial knowledge scope is documented.
* The target audience is defined.
* Source-selection requirements are established.
* Document metadata requirements are defined.
* The bilingual preparation approach is documented.
* Clinical approval requirements are clear.
* The remaining R05 checkpoints are defined.

## 13\. Important Governance Note

This plan is a project-level preparation document. It does not approve any medical content, establish source permissions, verify clinical reviewers, or authorize public deployment.

Those activities require their own evidence and approval.
