## 1. Purpose

This inventory records candidate authoritative publications and educational resources for the HIV/SRH Education Chatbot.

The documents have been identified from official Tanzanian government, WHO and UNFPA publication sources.

Inclusion in this inventory does not constitute approval for chatbot use. Each document must undergo source verification, permission assessment, language assessment and qualified clinical review before ingestion.

## 2. Verification Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Identified | A candidate publication or resource has been located. |
| Metadata verified | Title, publisher and publication details have been checked against the source record. |
| Full-text reviewed | The complete document has been inspected. |
| Permission verified | Reuse, storage, adaptation and translation rights have been assessed. |
| Clinical review pending | A qualified reviewer has not yet approved the content. |
| Approved | All required governance and clinical approval conditions have been satisfied. |
| Rejected | The document is unsuitable or fails mandatory requirements. |

Initial status for every candidate is **Identified**. Additional verification must be recorded separately.

## 6. Source and Permission Rules

1. Prefer original publisher pages and official repositories.
2. Do not rely on unofficial mirrors when an official source is available.
3. Record the exact edition and publication date.
4. Inspect licence terms for the specific document.
5. Check restrictions on third-party figures, tables and excerpts.
6. Do not assume that free access means permission to reproduce or redistribute.
7. Record permission evidence and the person who verified it.
8. Do not ingest a document solely because it appears in this inventory.

For WHO publications, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO terms may permit non-commercial copying, adaptation and translation subject to the licence. Each document's rights statement and third-party material must still be checked.
